Exposure data for every building on Earth.

OXM is an open model of the built environment. It links each building to its structure, use, occupants, and value. It works at global scale and at the level of a single building.

Open Data ↗ About us →
  • 250+ countries & regions
  • 3 billion buildings
  • 50+ TB of data
  • 2 / year releases
  • 20+ data sources
  • 10+ tools

Exposure has several layers.

Each building has a location, a shape, a use, and people inside it. It also sits in a hazard landscape.

A building is more than a footprint.
