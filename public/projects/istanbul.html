PROJECTS / EXAMPLES

Istanbul: exposure through historical layers

Model cultural, worship, and heritage buildings with changing occupancy, explicit cost concepts, and tasks that help mappers improve missing information.

Worked example · Heritage & occupancy

A place, its evidence, and a repeatable method.

01

Define the place

A self-contained place configuration brings together the study boundary, source exposure export, local inputs, and building classifications.

02

Represent time and value

Occupancy calendars and proximity shifts explore changes such as Friday prayer. Structural reconstruction, like-for-like replacement, art fabric, and heritage value are treated as distinct quantities.

03

Return gaps to the community

Completeness checks feed mapping priorities, while profiles, maps, figures, and a validation viewer make the assumptions and outputs inspectable.

What makes this case useful

The example connects a place’s cultural context to its exposure model. The workflow runs from input collection and inventory through building allocation, occupancy, cost, reporting, and validation. It offers a starting point for other heritage studies without treating one city’s parameters as universal.

Keep assumptions alongside the output.

Occupancy shifts and absolute cost rates require local evidence. The four cost concepts should not be summed into a single total. Generated outputs depend on the configured input export and the stages actually run.
