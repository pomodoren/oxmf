Albania: processing an area at a time

Divide a national workflow into twelve non-overlapping administrative areas, with a separate compute environment and output for each area.

Runnable configuration · Area processing

A place, its evidence, and a repeatable method.

01

Reuse country inputs

Load parent-country sources once, then define the individual study areas in a shared run configuration.

02

Process separate areas

Each area has an isolated compute database, its own export, and result metadata. The documented workflow coordinates the area runs.

03

Refine at the right scale

Replace the shared country model with calibrated area models when local evidence is available, while retaining the same processing structure.

From national sources to local runs

Area processing provides a practical way to organize larger studies. It keeps source preparation reusable and makes it possible to inspect, rerun, or improve one area without treating a country as a single indivisible job.

Keep assumptions alongside the output.

The bundled example applies a country-level OXM model to all twelve areas. Its outputs illustrate orchestration; area-specific calibration is needed before interpreting them as locally calibrated estimates.
