Durrës: exposure across the tourist season

Explore how a coastal city’s exposure changes with summer tourism, using accommodation-focused priorities and a locally configured occupancy model.

Configured example · Seasonal occupancy

A place, its evidence, and a repeatable method.

01

Keep the place self-contained

Durrës has its own configuration, small input files, occupancy controls, and output locations within the example collection.

02

Describe seasonal use

The coastal-tourism variant uses a summer-season occupancy swing and an accommodation-led priority profile to frame how buildings are used.

03

Adapt the evidence

Localize dates, resident-population controls, occupancy factors, and cost rates before producing a study for a new place.

A reusable method, with local parameters

The same sequence of collection, inventory, building allocation, occupancy, cost, and reporting can support different places. The common structure makes the differences in source evidence and local assumptions easier to discuss.

Keep assumptions alongside the output.

This is a configured example, not a published estimate of tourist numbers. Seasonal factors, population controls, and monetary rates need local calibration before operational interpretation.
